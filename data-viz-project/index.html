Is Social Media Causing Mental Health Issues?

A closer look into the differences in social media use and mental health symptoms across age groups

By Brenna Tichy

APRIL 13, 2026

This project compares social media use and mental health across age groups. The data shows that younger generations, especially Gen Z, spend more time on social media and also report higher rates of anxiety and depression. When viewed together, the graphs suggest a connection between increased screen time and poorer mental health among younger people.

Social media is not the only factor contributing to these trends. Academic pressure, economic concerns, and broader societal changes also influence mental health reports. However, the overlap between increased social media use and higher reported symptoms indicates it may be one part of the equation contributing to higher rates of diagnosed anxiety and depression among Gen Z.
